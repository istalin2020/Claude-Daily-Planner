Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

Daily Planner – My Day ("the App") is developed by istalin ("we", "us", "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App uses, where it goes, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

The App stores your data — tasks, notes, expenses, habits, meals, sleep logs, subscriptions and settings — locally on your device. We do not run accounts, and we do not collect or store your personal data on our own servers. The only data that leaves your device is described in sections 2, 4 and 5, and only when you use those features.

If you enable iCloud Sync (PRO feature), your data is synced through your personal iCloud account managed by Apple. We do not have access to your iCloud data. If you share a task list with someone, that list is sent to them through Apple's iCloud.

2. Gmail Expense Sync and Subscription Schedule (optional, PRO)

If you choose to connect a Gmail account, the App asks Google for read-only access (the gmail.readonly permission). It uses that access only to find bank transaction alerts, receipts and subscription emails, and to turn them into expenses and subscription entries for you to review.

  • Emails are read directly by the App on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or to any server of ours.
  • The transactions and subscriptions found are stored on your device (and in your own iCloud, if iCloud Sync is on). Your Google sign-in token is kept in the iOS Keychain on your device.
  • The App never sends, deletes, changes or labels email.
  • We do not use Gmail data for advertising, do not sell it, do not let people read it, and do not use it to train AI models.
  • You can disconnect at any time in the App (Gmail Expense Sync → Disconnect), or remove the App's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Apple HealthKit Data

The App integrates with Apple HealthKit to read and write the following data types, only after you tap Sync and allow it:

  • Read: Steps, active calories, exercise minutes, workouts, and sleep analysis
  • Write: Water intake and sleep records

We do not access, collect, store, or share any HealthKit data on external servers. HealthKit data is never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining purposes, and is not disclosed to any third party.

4. Smart Food Analysis (optional, PRO)

When you photograph a meal or type a dish name and ask for calories, the photo (reduced in size) or the dish name is sent over an encrypted connection to our analysis service on Cloudflare, which passes it to OpenAI to estimate calories and nutrients. The result is returned to your device.

  • We do not store your photos or dish names. OpenAI may keep API requests for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring, and does not use them to train its models.
  • A random ID created for this install (not your name, email or Apple ID) is kept for 48 hours to apply a fair daily usage limit.
  • If you don't use this feature, nothing is sent: free food logging works entirely on your device.

5. Subscriptions & Purchases

The App offers optional PRO subscriptions (monthly and yearly) through Apple's App Store. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. We do not collect or store any payment information, credit card details, or Apple ID credentials. To show app icons on the Subscriptions page, the App looks up app names in Apple's public App Store search.

6. Third-Party Services

The App does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites. The services involved are Apple (App Store, iCloud, HealthKit), Google (Gmail, only if you connect it), and Cloudflare and OpenAI (smart food analysis, only if you use it).

7. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored on your device using iOS standard storage, with your Google sign-in in the Keychain. If you delete the App, its locally stored data is removed. iCloud-synced data can be managed in your iCloud settings.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your Rights

Your data is on your device, so you have full control over it. You can delete it by deleting the App, disconnect Gmail at any time, and remove Apple Health access in the Health app. We keep no account or profile about you, so there is nothing on our side to export or delete.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
